Splash Isles Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026 · Social Power Hub LLC, Delaware, United States

Splash Isles is a virtual reality paint game for Meta Quest, made and run by Social Power Hub LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and how you can have it deleted. If you have questions, contact us at nalturk565@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

InformationWhere it comes from
Meta Horizon account information: your user ID and Horizon username (and profile photo, if shown)Meta Horizon, with the permissions you see when you start the game
Friends, invites, parties and block list: which of your Meta friends play the game, invites you send or accept, the party you are in, and the people you have blockedMeta Horizon
Game account and progress: a game account ID, the cosmetic items you own and wear (outfits, launcher skins, name tags), your settings, match results and rankingsCreated when you play

We do not collect your real name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos or camera images of your room, or any biometric data. Hand and head tracking is used only on the headset to move your avatar; we do not store it.

2. How we use it

  • To run the game: sign you in, match you with other players, connect you to game servers, and show your avatar, name tag and cosmetics to other players.
  • To keep your progress: remember the items you own, your outfit, settings and rankings between sessions and on a new headset.
  • To play with friends: show which friends are online, send and accept invites, and keep a party together in the same match.
  • To verify purchases: confirm with Meta that an item was bought before unlocking it.
  • To keep players safe: respect your block list, review reports, and act against players who break the rules (including banning a device from online play).
  • To fix and improve the game: find crashes and slow moments, using information that is aggregated or not linked to you.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising. There are no ads in Splash Isles.

3. Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that run parts of the game for us, and only what they need:

  • Meta Platforms: sign-in, friends, invites, parties and purchases (Meta's own privacy policy applies to your Meta account).
  • Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services): game accounts, saved progress and owned items, matchmaking, voice chat (Vivox), and downloading game content.
  • Edgegap: the game servers your matches and the hub island run on.

Other players see your Horizon username, avatar, cosmetics, name tag, team, scores and, if voice chat is on, your voice. We may also disclose information when the law requires it; we review every such request, challenge requests we believe are unlawful, disclose only the minimum required, and keep a record of each request.

4. Children

Splash Isles is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have, we delete it. If you are a parent and believe your child under 13 has played Splash Isles, contact us at nalturk565@gmail.com and we will delete their information.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your game account, owned items and settings while you play, and delete them after 24 months without play, or sooner if you ask. Purchase records are kept as long as the law requires for tax and accounting. Technical logs are kept for up to 90 days. Reports are kept as long as needed to review them and to enforce bans.

6. Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your data at any time, for free:

  1. Email nalturk565@gmail.com from any address, with the subject "Delete my Splash Isles data".
  2. Include your Horizon username, so we can find your account. We may ask you to confirm the request from inside the game.

We delete your game account, progress, owned-item records and settings within 30 days and confirm by email. We keep only what the law requires us to keep (for example purchase records for tax), and records needed to enforce an active ban, and we tell you if that applies. Deleting your data removes the cosmetic items you own in the game. Removing the game from your headset alone does not delete your data. You can also ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct it, the same way.

7. Security

Your information is sent encrypted between your headset and our services, and access to it is limited to the people who run the game.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the game.
